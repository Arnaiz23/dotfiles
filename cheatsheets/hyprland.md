# Hyprland

## Ventanas

| Atajo | Acción |
|---|---|
| SUPER + SHIFT + C | Cerrar ventana |
| SUPER + SHIFT + F | Fullscreen |
| SUPER + F | Toggle floating |
| SUPER + , | Monitor left |
| SUPER + . | Monitor right |

## Workspaces

| Atajo | Acción |
|---|---|
| SUPER + {1...12} | Workspace 1...12 |

## Custom Scripts

| Atajo | Acción |
|---|---|
| SUPER + SHIFT + Q | Power Rofi |
